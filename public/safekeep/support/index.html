from messages

  • in the chat, hold the photo and tap save.
  • hold the voice note and tap copy.
  • tap +, then safekeep, then add copied voice note.
  • tap the photo. done. the new copy is in photos → albums → safekeep.

from voice memos

  • open the memo, tap share, then safekeep.
  • tap the photo it belongs to.

hearing it

open the saved copy in photos and press and hold the picture. the voice note plays as the live photo's sound. make sure the ring/silent switch isn't muting it.

common questions

  • is my original photo changed? no. safekeep always saves a new copy.
  • paste is greyed out in messages. use "choose audio file", or open safekeep and paste there.
  • does anything get uploaded? no. see the privacy policy.
