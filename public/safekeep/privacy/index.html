privacy policy

nothing leaves your iphone.

effective september 28, 2026

safekeep collects no data. no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no servers. the app never sends your photos, voice notes, or anything else off your device.

what the app does on your device

  • you give safekeep a voice note (copied from messages, shared from voice memos, or picked from files) and choose a photo.
  • safekeep combines them into a new live photo copy and saves it to your photo library, in an album called "safekeep".
  • your original photo is never changed. the copy keeps the original's date and location details so it sits next to it in your library.
  • all of this processing happens on your iphone.

permissions

  • photos: used to show your recent photos so you can pick one, and to save the new copy. nothing is uploaded.
  • clipboard: read only when you tap paste, to get the voice note you copied.
  • microphone: only if you record a clip inside the app. the recording stays on your device.

data we collect

none. we don't collect, store, sell, or share personal information, and there are no third-party sdks in the app. if you use icloud photos, apple syncs your library under apple's own privacy policy, not ours.

children

safekeep doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

changes

if this ever changes, this page will be updated first, with a new effective date.
