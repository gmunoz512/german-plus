privacy policy

nothing leaves your browser.

effective october 14, 2026

empsize collects no data. no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no servers. the extension makes no network requests.

what the extension does

  • it reads the text on the page you're viewing and bolds the start of each word, right in your browser.
  • page text is never stored, logged, or sent anywhere.
  • it leaves code, inputs and links alone.

permissions

  • storage: saves your settings (on/off, per-site choices, bold amount). if your browser syncs extension settings, apple or google syncs them under their own privacy policy, not ours.
  • all websites: needed so it can bold text on any page you turn it on for. it only changes how text looks.

data we collect

none. we don't collect, store, sell, or share personal information, and there are no third-party libraries that phone home.

changes

if this ever changes, this page will be updated first, with a new effective date.
