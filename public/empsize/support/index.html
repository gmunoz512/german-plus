turning it on

  • safari (iphone, ipad): settings → apps → safari → extensions → empsize. switch it on and allow on all websites.
  • safari (mac): safari → settings → extensions, then check empsize.
  • chrome: pin empsize from the puzzle menu, then click it to flip the switch.

common questions

  • nothing is bolding. open the empsize menu and check the switch for that site, then reload the page.
  • too much or too little bold? adjust the bold amount in the empsize menu.
  • does anything get uploaded? no. see the privacy policy.
